About us

Hardware security
at IMSE-CNM (CSIC)

The security of modern computing infrastructure relies on hardware. At HWSec-CSIC we explore advanced, open approaches to secure systems, including RISC-V architectures and integrated photonics.

We are based at the Instituto de Microelectrónica de Sevilla, a joint centre of CSIC and the Universidad de Sevilla.
